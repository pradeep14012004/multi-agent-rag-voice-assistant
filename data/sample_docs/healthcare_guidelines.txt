HEALTHCARE: BASIC PATIENT SAFETY EDUCATION

Hand hygiene is an important infection prevention practice. A basic handwashing procedure includes wetting hands with clean running water, applying soap, rubbing all hand surfaces, rinsing, and drying with a clean towel. Follow the local clinical protocol for the required duration and circumstances.

Patient identification helps ensure that care, medication, tests, and procedures are provided to the correct person. Healthcare workers should use the approved identifiers and institutional verification process before providing care. Do not rely only on room number or bed location.

Clear communication between healthcare professionals supports continuity of care. Important information should be documented accurately and shared through approved clinical processes.

This sample file is educational demonstration content only. It is not a clinical guideline and must not be used as medical advice.
